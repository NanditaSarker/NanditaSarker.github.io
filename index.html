About me

I am a DrPH candidate in the Department of Environmental and Occupational Health at the Texas A&M University School of Public Health, with an expected graduation in Summer 2027. My work focuses on environmental epidemiology, exposure assessment and biomonitoring, and human health risk assessment.

Before my doctoral studies, I was an Assistant Professor in the Department of Environmental Sciences at Jahangirnagar University and a Lecturer at Noakhali Science and Technology University in Bangladesh. I hold an M.S. and a B.Sc. in Environmental Sciences from Jahangirnagar University.
