Research

My research examines environmental exposures, their sources, and the health risks they pose.

Community science and environmental health training

Since August 2026, I have been a Graduate Research Assistant with Together for a Better Tomorrow (TBT) at the Texas A&M School of Public Health, on a project funded by the National Academies of Sciences, Engineering, and Medicine (about $1M).

  • Developing and implementing a Community Science and Environmental Health Training Program with community partners (Objective 1).
  • Conducting literature reviews and evidence synthesis to inform program development, evaluation, and community engagement.
  • Supporting programmatic activities that build community capacity to collect and interpret soil and water data.

Environmental health and safety

From June to December 2024, I was an Environmental Health and Safety Intern on the Laboratory Building Operations Team at Texas A&M University.

  • Conducted and shadowed laboratory safety inspections and hazardous waste management procedures.
  • Restructured and expanded the EHS Laboratory Safety Manual using a risk assessment and management framework.
  • Developed and updated laboratory safety and cryogenics training, high-hazard chemical SOPs, and new PI safety materials.
